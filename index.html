The product

One trip. Fewer information gaps.

Air travel information is often scattered across airline apps, airport pages, weather products, family text threads, and terminal signage. AeroSync brings the planning decisions into one clear workflow.

AeroSync does not pretend to be an airline or government system. It labels sources, distinguishes live observations from planning estimates, and keeps the user focused on the next practical action.

01

Connection Risk

Scores layovers using time, airport complexity, terminal transfers, baggage, traveler needs, and current operating conditions.

02

Pickup Coordination

Builds a practical plan for when to leave, where to wait, and what family members should communicate before entering airport traffic.

03

International Context

Supports major global hubs, international connection buffers, optional location context, and airport map views.

04

Smart Travel Packet

Turns trip details, risk results, pickup instructions, and backup steps into a shareable travel packet.

05

Airline Markets

Tracks public airline stocks in a separate research view with clearly labeled market-data timing and financial disclaimers.

06

Local-First Profile

Keeps travel planning simple with local trip preferences, saved plans, and no required AeroSync account.

Live data architecture

Real information, with honest boundaries.

Automatic

Current airport conditions

Official Aviation Weather Center METAR reports refresh while relevant app screens are open. No API key is required.

Optional

Flight-specific details

An authorized aviation provider can add exact flight status, estimated arrival, terminal, gate, and baggage information when available.

Explicit

No fabricated operations data

If an operational source is unavailable, AeroSync says so. Planning estimates are never presented as official airline status.

Our data standard

Every operational view should answer three questions:

  1. Where did this information come from?
  2. When was it last updated?
  3. What should the traveler do next?

Research & policy · YIP fellowship capstone

Clearer information. More confident travelers.

In his Institute for Youth in Policy fellowship work, AeroSync founder Adhvaith Ananth examines how fragmented airline, airport, and security information affects passengers. His policy brief and legislative proposal explore a coordinated approach to delays, connections, security preparation, accessibility, and airport pickups.

Consistent passenger information

The proposal calls for common communication categories across existing airline and airport systems, covering delays, gates, baggage, connections, and passenger rights.

Preparation before the checkpoint

The brief recommends integrating official TSA guidance into trip communication so travelers can prepare for identification checks, carry-on rules, and assistance needs.

Accessible communication

The proposal emphasizes accessible formats, language access, and ways to receive important information beyond smartphone apps. Continuous passenger location tracking would not be required.

A pilot that can be evaluated

A proposed three-year pilot at at least five major airports would evaluate passenger confusion, connection issues, security preparation, and pickup congestion, with DFW suggested as a participant.
